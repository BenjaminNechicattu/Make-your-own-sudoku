MYOS Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 7, 2026

This policy describes how the MYOS Android app, Make Your Own Sudoku, handles information. MYOS is developed by Benjamin G Nechicattu.

Information stored by MYOS

MYOS stores information you enter and create in the app on your device. This can include your player name, Sudoku boards and notes, game progress, completed-puzzle history, streaks, and appearance preferences. The app uses this information to provide its features.

MYOS does not send this app data to the developer. It does not require an account and does not include advertising, analytics, or tracking services.

Storage, backup, and deletion

App data is stored in MYOS's private storage on your device. Android may include app data in a device backup if backup is enabled in your device settings; any such backup is managed by Android and your backup provider, not by MYOS. You can erase MYOS data using Reset app data in Settings, by clearing the app's storage in Android settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Links and platform services

MYOS can open the developer's website in your browser. If you follow that link, the website and browser handle the resulting visit under their own privacy practices. Android and Google Play may also process app installation, diagnostics, or backup information under their own terms and privacy policies.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be published at this page with a revised effective date.
